# Tech debt

Known shortcuts, deferred work, and things left deliberately unfinished — logged so they're a choice, not a surprise. Format: what/why/impact/where. Remove an entry once it's actually fixed rather than leaving it marked done.

**Project WhatsApp groups: no Drive copy, retries wait for the next message, backfill is the last 100.**
What: since DECISIONS #193, photos, videos and documents from a linked WhatsApp group are copied into the job's files in app storage, even when the company keeps its files in Google Drive (an upload made in the CRM moves to Drive; this copy doesn't). A copy that fails is retried only when the same group's next message arrives, three tries in all, so a group that goes quiet keeps a failed photo waiting. Linking a group reads its last 100 messages and no further back, and a file from an old message may already be gone from WhatsApp by then. Messages from groups on no project are kept, unread by anyone, until the group is linked. Why: the first version keeps the copy inside one webhook's `after()`, with no new cron. Impact: a Drive company finds WhatsApp photos in the CRM but not in its Drive folder; rarely, a photo waits for the group's next message. Fix: run the copy through `recordLeadFile`'s Drive step, and add a sweep of `media_status = 'pending'` rows to an existing cron. Where: `src/lib/whatsapp-company.ts` (`saveGroupMedia`, `backfillGroup`), `src/app/api/whatsapp/webhook/route.ts`.

**A report can stay faded if a link to the page it already shows lands while its own period is loading.**
What: on the Schedule, Text Reports and Appointment Reports, pick a period and, before it loads, follow a link to the address the report already shows (the sidebar's item for that page, a bare Daily Brief tile). The router drops the pick, the address doesn't move, so nothing is followed and nothing is asked again: the list stays faded under the picked period until another is picked. Why: telling that from the pick still being on its way needs the router's own word that the request was dropped, which it doesn't give; guessing from the transition's pending flag was judged too fragile. The old rule behaved the same. Impact: rare (two clicks within one page load), and one more pick clears it. Fix: if Next exposes navigation completion, follow the loaded address when the view's own request ends without landing. Where: `src/lib/report-address.ts`, `src/app/(app)/schedule/schedule-list.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/text-reports/text-reports-view.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/appointment-reports/appointment-reports-view.tsx`.

**A sales-rep commission released by recording job costs is dated by its payment or certificate, not the release.**
What: a sales-rep line has three holds: final payment, completion certificate, and job costs recorded. `commissionQualifiedAt` dates it by the later of the first two only, since costs have no single "recorded" moment. So a job paid off Sep 10, signed off Sep 12 and first costed Oct 5 qualifies on Sep 12. On October's statement it sits in the opening balance, owed, rather than in "Payable this period". Why: no column records when a job's costs first arrived, so the date rule leaves that hold out. Impact: such a line is listed as payable on an earlier statement's dates. With the payout ledger its money still carries forward; without it, only an earlier period's statement lists it. Fix: record when the costs hold cleared (the first `job_expenses` or bill on the contract) and take the latest of the three. Where: `src/lib/data/types.ts` (`commissionQualifiedAt`), `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts`.

**Customer financing is a link to the lender, not a connection.**
What: since DECISIONS #161 a company adds its lender's application link and customers see Apply for financing in the portal, but nothing comes back from the lender: since #162 the office records each step on the estimate by hand (applied, approved, declined, funded), since #163 Funded can record the payout as Financing payments on the contract (and since #169 the lender's fee as a job cost), since #164 a link sent or an application in puts a follow-up task on the person's list, since #166 a signed contract can be switched to financing with a payment change the customer signs, and since #168 an estimate can be financed through the customer's own lender, named by hand (nothing comes back from that lender either). Since #170 a company can list several lenders and move a declined customer to the next one; nothing comes back from any of them. While a contract is paying with financing, its bills read Financing on Invoices and Money to Collect and ring no overdue alert (#167), but the dashboard's overdue-money card (its rollup) and the customer statement's "past due" still count a stage billed before the switch by its due date. Signing the payment change doesn't alert the office; the estimate's Financing panel shows it. Why: a real connection needs AI Build Pros to be accepted as a software partner by a lender (Wisetack, Hearth and others), which only the owner can apply for. Impact: the office hears from the lender and enters each step by hand. Fix: once a partnership exists, create the application from the estimate, take the lender's status updates, and record the payout. Where: `src/lib/financing.ts`, `src/app/portal/estimates/[id]/financing-offer.tsx`.

**QuickBooks: bills, invoices, customer payments and lender fees go; other "Already paid" job costs are entered by hand.**
What: since DECISIONS #173, #174 and #184, bills, bill payments and receipts, and invoices, deposits, customer payments, credits and refunds go to a company's QuickBooks once it turns each on; since #199 lender fees go too, as expenses; other job costs saved as "Already paid" (before bills existed) are entered by hand from the start date (the CRM never recorded what paid them). By hand for now: bills to customers with sales tax and what's paid on them (a stage's share of a contract's tax isn't stored), refunds the customer owes again and a payment made again after one (QuickBooks can't reopen a paid invoice from outside), credit change orders (a billed stage below zero), job costs below zero or dated in closed books, money paid on the side of a change order that didn't go to QuickBooks, money paid before the start date, anything dated in a month QuickBooks' books have closed, what went to QuickBooks for a contact later deleted in the CRM, what the office was told to enter by hand (it stays so: the CRM can't see whether it was; a "take it out of QuickBooks" note for one deleted in the CRM since has no button to clear it yet), Stripe's fees and payouts (the CRM doesn't record them), card disputes. A customer, job or vendor is never renamed or changed once added; a bill's Bill no. is empty (no vendor invoice number in the CRM); changing an account match or product isn't applied to what's already sent; a bill the bookkeeper split into lines can't have its total changed from the CRM, nor an invoice's lines or amount after it went (only its dates); a payment with no "paid from" account waits until it's deleted and recorded again. A receipt QuickBooks doesn't take (a .heic or .webp photo the browser didn't convert) or one kept in Google Drive waits until it's attached again; a receipt the bookkeeper deletes in QuickBooks isn't sent again unless replaced. The QuickBooks login is refreshed whenever the jobs use it, so only a company with all three off can let it run out (about 100 days) and connect again. Each run reads every bill since the start dates (the job-costs run, every "Already paid" cost since its start date) and every `quickbooks_sync` row: fine at hundreds a month, worth narrowing (a changed-since mark) at thousands. Step 3 was tested against a stand-in for QuickBooks, not yet on Intuit's practice company; automatic sales tax can't be fully tried there either: the invoice total check is the guard. Also: a paid bill can't be moved onto or off a job without removing its payments first, and Field/Production can't correct a bill payment they entered — only Bills to Pay can. A lender fee and its payout aren't linked: deleting or changing a payout leaves its fee (in the CRM and QuickBooks); a loan paid in draws gets one fee per draw. A job cost's receipt kept in Google Drive isn't attached (the CRM can't download from Drive), and one replaced while Drive is connected leaves the old attachment in QuickBooks. A job cost deleted after it was told by hand is named in Needs a look only by its customer. A fee saved by Funded before 0230 that the backfill doesn't catch is entered by hand. A job-costs start date set too early gives the old costs from it lasting by-hand notes, with no warning on the card and no button to clear them (as step 3). A customer deleted and restored within the same second as a run's reads, a restore that fails to put a cost back, or a cost moved to another customer with ✎ Edit whose new customer is deleted before the next run notices the move, reads as a cost deleted with ✎ Edit (its expense is deleted in QuickBooks); a trash entry left behind by a restore whose clean-up failed makes Edit deletes for that customer wait until it's purged (30 days). If QuickBooks' books are closed between a run's closing-date read and a fee's delete, its receipt can come off before the delete is refused. A deleted customer's records that were waiting or refused stay as they are and aren't counted. Expenses were tested against a stand-in for QuickBooks, not yet on Intuit's practice company (Open in QuickBooks' expense address, sparse changes and deletes unconfirmed). Why: one step at a time, as the owner approved. Impact: taxed customer bills and old "Already paid" costs are still typed into QuickBooks. Where: `src/lib/quickbooks/` (`bill-sync.ts`, `bill-sync-run.ts`, `invoice-sync.ts`, `invoice-sync-run.ts`, `cost-sync.ts`, `cost-status.ts`, `cost-sync-run.ts`), `supabase/migrations/0176_bill_payment_accounts.sql`, `0222_quickbooks_bills.sql`, `0223_quickbooks_receipts.sql`, `0227_quickbooks_invoices.sql`, `0230_quickbooks_job_costs.sql`, `src/lib/actions/vendor-bills.ts`.
