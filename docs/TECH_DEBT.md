# Tech debt

Known shortcuts, deferred work, and things left deliberately unfinished — logged so they're a choice, not a surprise. Format: what/why/impact/where. Remove an entry once it's actually fixed rather than leaving it marked done.

**A report can stay faded if a link to the page it already shows lands while its own period is loading.**
What: on the Schedule, Text Reports and Appointment Reports, pick a period and, before it loads, follow a link to the address the report already shows (the sidebar's item for that page, a bare Daily Brief tile). The router drops the pick, the address doesn't move, so nothing is followed and nothing is asked again: the list stays faded under the picked period until another is picked. Why: telling that from the pick still being on its way needs the router's own word that the request was dropped, which it doesn't give; guessing from the transition's pending flag was judged too fragile. The old rule behaved the same. Impact: rare (two clicks within one page load), and one more pick clears it. Fix: if Next exposes navigation completion, follow the loaded address when the view's own request ends without landing. Where: `src/lib/report-address.ts`, `src/app/(app)/schedule/schedule-list.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/text-reports/text-reports-view.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/appointment-reports/appointment-reports-view.tsx`.

**The Estimates and Contracts date filters and the rep report's Custom dates use the UTC day.**
What: both dashboards, Marketing Analytics, the rep report's numbers, Text Reports, Profit & Loss and the commission statements go by the company's day (0224–0226, DECISIONS #175–#179). These places are known to still use the UTC day, which starts at 5pm Pacific (4pm in winter):
- **The Estimates and Contracts date filters**, and the funnel cards they narrow, compare `created_at` by its UTC day (`withinWindow(e.created_at, …)`). A document made after 5pm on a range's last day is left out, and one from the evening before the range starts is counted.
- **The rep report's Custom range** seeds its date boxes from the browser's UTC date (`report-filters.tsx`). On the evening of the 31st, pressing Custom shows next month's 1st.

Why: each is its own page, left for its own change. Impact: counts at the edges of a period are off by an evening, and a seeded date can read a day ahead.

Fix: check each timestamp with `stampedWithin(win, zone)` (as Text Reports, the rep report and the commission statements do), and seed date boxes from the company's today.

Where: `src/app/(app)/estimates/estimates-view.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/contracts/contracts-view.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/marketing-analytics/rep-report/report-filters.tsx`.

**Customer financing is a link to the lender, not a connection.**
What: since DECISIONS #161 a company adds its lender's application link and customers see Apply for financing in the portal, but nothing comes back from the lender: since #162 the office records each step on the estimate by hand (applied, approved, declined, funded), since #163 Funded can record the payout as Financing payments on the contract (and since #169 the lender's fee as a job cost), since #164 a link sent or an application in puts a follow-up task on the person's list, since #166 a signed contract can be switched to financing with a payment change the customer signs, and since #168 an estimate can be financed through the customer's own lender, named by hand (nothing comes back from that lender either). Since #170 a company can list several lenders and move a declined customer to the next one; nothing comes back from any of them. While a contract is paying with financing, its bills read Financing on Invoices and Money to Collect and ring no overdue alert (#167), but the dashboard's overdue-money card (its rollup) and the customer statement's "past due" still count a stage billed before the switch by its due date. Signing the payment change doesn't alert the office; the estimate's Financing panel shows it. Why: a real connection needs AI Build Pros to be accepted as a software partner by a lender (Wisetack, Hearth and others), which only the owner can apply for. Impact: the office hears from the lender and enters each step by hand. Fix: once a partnership exists, create the application from the estimate, take the lender's status updates, and record the payout. Where: `src/lib/financing.ts`, `src/app/portal/estimates/[id]/financing-offer.tsx`.

**QuickBooks: bills go; invoices, customer payments and job costs don't yet.**
What: since DECISIONS #173, bills and bill payments go to a company's QuickBooks once it turns sending on, with their receipts (#174); customers, invoices and customer payments are step 3 and job costs step 4. Until then: job costs saved as "Already paid" before bills existed, and financing lender fees, aren't sent (they aren't bills); costs aren't tagged to the job as a QuickBooks customer, only named in the memo; QuickBooks' Bill no. is empty because the CRM has no vendor invoice number; changing an account match isn't applied to bills already sent (a change keeps the line's account in QuickBooks); a bill the bookkeeper split into several lines in QuickBooks can't have its total changed from the CRM; a payment with no "paid from" account waits until it's deleted and recorded again (payments can't be edited). The 0176 placeholders `vendor_bills.qb_bill_id`, `vendor_bill_payments.qb_payment_id`, `qb_synced_at` and `vendors.qb_vendor_id` stay unused (`quickbooks_sync` replaced them). A receipt QuickBooks doesn't take (a .heic or .webp photo the browser didn't convert) or one kept in Google Drive waits until it's attached again; the CRM doesn't convert it. A receipt the bookkeeper deletes in QuickBooks isn't sent again unless it's replaced in the CRM. The QuickBooks login is refreshed whenever the job uses it, so only a company with sending off can still let it run out after about 100 days (Intuit's limit) and connect again. Each run reads every bill since the start date and every `quickbooks_sync` row for the company: fine at hundreds of bills a month, worth narrowing (a changed-since mark) at thousands. Also: a paid bill can't be moved onto or off a job without removing its payments first, and Field/Production can't correct a bill payment they entered — only Bills to Pay can. Why: one step at a time, as the owner approved. Impact: invoices and payments are still typed into QuickBooks by hand. Where: `src/lib/quickbooks/bill-sync.ts`, `bill-sync-run.ts`, `supabase/migrations/0176_bill_payment_accounts.sql`, `0222_quickbooks_bills.sql`, `0223_quickbooks_receipts.sql`, `src/lib/actions/vendor-bills.ts`.

**Lead cost is dollars, not cents.**
What: `leads.lead_cost` (0023), `company_profile.default_lead_cost` (0089) and `lead_sources.default_lead_cost` (0166) are `numeric` dollars while every other money column is integer cents — `marketing_spend.amount_cents` (0165), on the same settings page, is cents. **A CSV import chunk whose response is lost may be re-sent on Resume.**
What: the import uploads 2,000-row chunks and advances its resume cursor only after a chunk's response arrives; if the connection drops after the server inserted a chunk but before the browser heard back, Resume sends that chunk again and duplicates up to 2,000 rows. Why: true exactly-once would need an idempotency key per chunk and a server-side ledger — real plumbing for a narrow window that only opens on a mid-request connection loss. Impact: rare; visible immediately (contact count runs ~2,000 high) and recoverable by deleting the doubled rows or re-running the duplicate check on a fresh import. Where: `src/app/(app)/pipeline/csv-import-panel.tsx` (`runImport`), `src/lib/actions/leads.ts` (`bulkImportLeads`).

**Assistant voice input is only as good as the browser's speech engine.**
What: the mic on the AI assistant uses the Web Speech API, so recognition quality, language handling and availability are whatever the person's browser ships — Chrome/Edge/Safari carry it, Firefox doesn't (the mic simply doesn't render there), and a loud job site may transcribe poorly with no knob for us to turn. **The AI receptionist pauses between turns and can't be interrupted.**

**The Dispatch Dashboard's panels don't drag-arrange, and its fallback scans 90 days of pre-appointment leads.**
What: the main Dashboard's boxes drag to a saved order (`profiles.dashboard_panel_order`); the Dispatch Dashboard's five panels render in a fixed order, and its desk-table names link to the unfiltered Call Reports page (that page takes no rep or disposition in its URL). Until migration 0171 is run (and again until 0225 is, since the app now sends the zone), the fallback path reads every pre-appointment lead of the last 90 days (three slim columns, paged) to bucket the waiting panel — the RPC replaces that scan. Why: a second order column and a shared drag hook were more than the page's first cut warranted, and Call Reports' filters are in-page state. Impact: a dispatcher cannot move the desk table to the top; a click from an outcome row lands on the whole call log. Fix: generalize the panel-order plumbing (a keyed column or a jsonb map) and give Call Reports `?rep=` / `?disposition=` query params; run 0171 and 0225 so the fallback never runs.

**Google Calendar sync is polled, not pushed.**
What: changes made in Google reach the CRM on the next 15-minute cron run (or Sync now), not instantly; Google's push channels (`events.watch`) are not used. Why: channels need a public endpoint, renewal every few days and a channel id per connection — worth it only if the delay ever bites. Impact: a rep who moves an appointment in Google and then looks at the CRM inside the same 15 minutes sees the old slot. Where: `src/lib/google-calendar/sync.ts`, `src/app/api/cron/google-calendar-sync/route.ts`.


**Live location stops when the CRM leaves the screen (browser only).**
What: in a phone browser the location sharer stops reporting when the phone locks or the tab is in the background. Why: only a native app may track in the background. Impact: anyone clocked in from a browser instead of the phone app shows "Location off" on the Team Map with the phone in a pocket. The cure is the phone app (decision #074); once it's in the stores, the Time Clock could nudge browser users to install it. Where: `src/app/(app)/location-sharer.tsx`.

**An approved week can't take a forgotten shift without reopening it.**
What: since DECISIONS #157 a person's week is approved on Timesheets and its punches are then locked in the database. The person sees whether each week was approved on their Time Clock page (1.240.0), but nothing tells them the moment it happens (no text or notification), and an approved week can't be changed even to add a forgotten shift -- reopen it first. Why: the owner asked for the lock before export; an office "Add missing time" is a separate piece of work (it's also what Must-be-on-site mode waits on). Impact: a forgotten shift in an approved week takes a reopen, a fix and a second approval. Where: `src/app/(app)/timesheets/`, `src/lib/actions/timesheet-approvals.ts`, `supabase/migrations/0212_timesheet_approvals.sql`.

**Office edits to hours are audited in the action, not by a trigger.**
What: `correctPunch` writes `time_punch_changes`; an Office/Admin user editing `time_punches` directly through the Supabase API would bypass the history (RLS allows it — the audit does not). Why: the action is the only writer the app has; a trigger would also fire on the cron's auto clock-out and the worker's own clock-out. Impact: none through the app. The cure if needed: a `before update` trigger that records only updates by Office/Admin. Where: `src/lib/actions/time-clock.ts`, `supabase/migrations/0174_time_clock.sql`.

**The phone app is untested on a device and has no store listing.**
What: `mobile/` was generated and configured without an Android SDK or a Mac, so the first device test is the owner's (GitHub Actions builds the Android APK and the signed Play bundle); iOS has never been built. The move to API 36 (#086) has also only been proven on CI, never on a phone. Why: the sandbox cannot download Android build tools and has no Xcode; the Apple account doesn't exist yet. Impact: nothing until someone installs it. The cure: install the CI-built APK on an Android 15+ phone and run a shift. Check the CRM sits below the status bar and that the keyboard doesn't hide the field being typed in. Build iOS on a GitHub Mac runner once there is an Apple account (`docs/MOBILE_RELEASE.md`). Where: `mobile/`, `.github/workflows/android-app.yml`, `.github/workflows/android-release.yml`.

**Invoices: Drive receipts may not open for customers.**
What: since DECISIONS #149 an invoice can wait as a draft and be edited until it's issued; after that there's no edit (cancel and re-issue instead), by design. Since DECISIONS #150 an invoice goes by text, email (with its PDF) or both. A billed cost's receipt kept in Google Drive (`receipt_path` `drive:…`) links to Drive, which a customer can open only if the file is shared. The Drive backup files an invoice's PDF under "Contracts" (it's status Signed). Why: the first cut covers the permit-fee case the owner asked for. Impact: typos cost a cancel and a new number. Fix: an "Invoices" category in `docCategory` (`src/lib/actions/lead-files.ts`). Since DECISIONS #148 the Invoices page lists every bill with its status; since #150 a bill sent by text or email reads "Sent" and one only marked billed reads "Billed" -- bills sent before 0206 ran read "Billed", since nothing recorded those sends. The page and Money to Collect also compute every status in the server from the company's billed stages and their payments -- slim rows, a few thousand for a busy company after years, but read whole on each visit; a database view of what is owed per stage would be the cure if that ever grows large. Since DECISIONS #151 the customer is emailed a receipt when money arrives (online automatically, by hand when asked). A receipt is an email only, with no PDF copy, and a company whose own Stripe account also emails receipts sends two unless it switches the CRM's off; until 0207 runs nothing is receipted automatically. A refund notice (DECISIONS #158) goes only when the office asks -- the Refund form's checkbox, or Email refund notice on the refund's row -- never by itself; a refund made in Stripe may also get Stripe's own email if the Stripe account sends them. Since DECISIONS #152 a company can switch on automatic payment reminders: the schedule is fixed (3 days before, the day, weekly up to 3 times; 9am-6pm company time), and they read only bills due within the last 60 days, so a bill more than ~2 months late is the office's to chase. Since DECISIONS #153 each customer has a statement; since #159 it can cover a period, opening on the balance before it. A payment kept on a cancelled contract shows as a credit until refunds are recorded (step 6). The period quick picks are calendar years only (no This month or quarter), and a statement's opening balance is worked out from all of the customer's history each time it's shown -- fine for one customer's bills, even over years. Since DECISIONS #154 a bill can be credited, and since #160 a credit given by hand can be removed (it stays on record, marked removed); a deposit can't be credited (it isn't a bill -- void the contract or refund it). A removed credit is left off the customer's statement entirely, so a customer who saw it on an earlier statement isn't told it was taken back; the office tells them. Since DECISIONS #155 refunds are recorded: in the office's own screens (Invoices, Money to Collect, Payments) a bill with a Stripe refund nobody has answered "still owed?" for reads owed again until someone does (the customer's side is held as it was); a Stripe refund the CRM refused (the same money also recorded by hand) shows up only in Sync payments from Stripe' notes; refunds older than 180 days that the webhook missed aren't caught up; and a deposit part-refunded still reads paid while the statement shows the refunded part as owed (a deposit can't be credited). Where: `src/lib/actions/invoices.ts`, `src/components/invoices/new-invoice-modal.tsx`, `src/lib/data/load-invoice-rows.ts`, `src/lib/send-receipt.ts`.

**File deletion history misses cascades, and app-storage deletes can't be undone.**
What: `lead_file_deletions` (0182) is written by `deleteLeadFile` only. Files removed because their lead is deleted (`on delete cascade`) leave no entry, and the lead's own history rows go with it. A file kept in app storage (not Google Drive) is removed from the bucket immediately, with no trash. Why: single-file delete is the case the owner asked about, and Drive's trash covers the companies that keep files there. Impact: a deleted lead's files can't be traced, and a mis-deleted app-storage photo is gone. Fix: a `before delete` trigger on `lead_files` for the trail (service-role paths have `auth.uid()` null), and a soft-delete (`deleted_at`) with a purge job for app storage. Where: `src/lib/actions/lead-files.ts`, `supabase/migrations/0182_lead_file_deletions.sql`.

**Jobs finished before 1.165.0 keep their old stored status outside the Production Board.**
What: the board works out Complete / On Hold / Cancelled from the documents at load (#088), and new certificates, holds and drags write `jobs.status` too. A certificate signed before 1.165.0 never wrote the row, and a voided contract never changes it. Why: fixing the board needed no SQL, and a one-off data fix wasn't asked for. Impact: the Dashboard's production counts and the Schedule/Calendar job lists may still show those jobs as Not Started or In Progress. Fix: a one-off idempotent `update jobs set status = 'Complete'` joined to signed completion certificates on the lead's live contract, or read `boardPlacement` in those views too. Where: `src/lib/actions/dashboard.ts`, `src/app/(app)/schedule/page.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/calendar/page.tsx`, `src/lib/production-board.ts`.


**Marketing Analytics matches bought lists by exact spelling; the rest of the CRM doesn't.**
What: the lead rule (DECISIONS #156) compares sources ignoring case and surrounding spaces, but Marketing Analytics' *Exclude bought lists* still passes the ticked names to `marketing_analytics_rollup` as exact text, and it doesn't drop sourceless contacts (its "Unknown" row). Why: that rollup groups by exact source text throughout, and changing its matching is its own migration. Impact: a contact whose source is "csv import" while Settings says "CSV Import", or one with no source, still counts in Marketing's lead figures while the dashboard leaves it out. Fix: give `marketing_analytics_rollup` the `counts_as_lead` filter (0211) instead of `p_exclude_sources`.

**Sourceless contacts still get the default lead cost; the phone's lead list has no Lead tag.**
What: 0211 prices a bought-list contact at $0 but leaves a contact with no source at the company default, though it isn't counted as a lead; and the phone-width Pipeline list shows the source as plain text, without the desktop card's orange "Lead · …" tag (its Leads only filter works). Why: only bought lists were asked about, and the phone card line is a joined text string (`leadCardMeta`). Impact: a hand-entered contact without a source shows $375 on its own Lead Cost and in Marketing's "Unknown" row (the rep report already leaves it out); on a phone, telling leads from list contacts takes the filter.

**The iPhone app has no Speaker button.**
What: the dialer's Speaker button (DECISIONS #111) comes from the Android app's own CallAudio plugin, and the iPhone app has no Swift counterpart, so on iPhone the button is hidden (DECISIONS #171). Why: the TestFlight pipeline came first, and the plugin can't be tested without an iPhone build to install. Impact: iPhone users can't switch a CRM call to the loudspeaker from the dialer. Fix: a Swift `CallAudio` plugin with `isSpeakerOn`/`setSpeaker` over `AVAudioSession.overrideOutputAudioPort`, registered in the app, plus a name/method test like `call-audio.test.ts`.
