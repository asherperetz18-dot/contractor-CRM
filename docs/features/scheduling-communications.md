| AI receptionist | shipped | When a call would hit voicemail — no forwarding number, or the forwarded phone rang out — the AI answers instead (per-company toggle, off by default, migration 0160): turn-based over Twilio `<Gather>` speech + Claude + `<Say>` (Polly neural voice), it introduces itself as an AI, takes name/project/address/callback window, never quotes a price or promises a firm appointment, then files everything — lead matched by caller ID or created through the same race-guarded RPC as CallRail, a 🤖 note with the full transcript, the call log marked "AI Receptionist", and a confirmation text. It can also **book the visit in pencil** (decision #043): a concrete agreed day becomes a real event — unassigned, unconfirmed, "Estimate" — the lead advances to Appointment Scheduled, and the text says "reply YES to confirm", which the SMS webhook's existing yes/no matching honors; dates resolve against the company's own timezone, never the server's, and the pure validator refuses the past, >60 days out, and absurd hours. It can **transfer to a person** (decision #044): with a transfer number configured (migration 0161), a caller who asks for someone — or presses 0, since the gather accepts DTMF too — gets `<Dial>`ed through; a human answering ends the AI's session, nobody answering resumes the AI mid-call, so a failed transfer is never a dead end, and the transcript note shows the attempt either way. Conversation policy is a pure tested module; every webhook failure degrades to a spoken apology or the old voicemail path, never a dead line. Finalize runs instantly on a clean goodbye (`after()`), with a sweep on each inbound call plus a 2-hourly cron as the floor for mid-call hangups. | `src/lib/ai-receptionist.ts`, `src/lib/ai-receptionist-engine.ts`, `src/app/api/voice/ai/turn/`, `src/app/api/voice/ai/transfer/`, `src/app/api/voice/inbound/`, `src/app/api/cron/ai-receptionist-finalize/`, `src/app/(app)/settings/ai-receptionist/`, `supabase/migrations/0160_ai_receptionist.sql`, `supabase/migrations/0161_receptionist_transfer.sql` |
