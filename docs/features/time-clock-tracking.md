# Time Clock & Tracking

Clocking in and out, live location while on the clock, automatic arrivals at appointments and production jobs, the location check at clock-in, and the hours that go to payroll. Migrations `0174_time_clock.sql` and `0185_clock_in_check.sql`; decisions #073 and #095.

| Feature | Status | Description | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time Clock | shipped | Everyone in a tracked role (Settings; default Sales, Field, Production, Office) clocks in, takes breaks and clocks out from their phone. First use shows a location notice that must be accepted. Shows hours today (a live stopwatch), where they are ("At Roof inspection · logged automatically") and today's timeline and schedule. A break ends the current punch, so breaks are unpaid without break arithmetic. | `src/app/(app)/time-clock/`, `src/lib/actions/time-clock.ts`, `src/lib/time-clock/hours.ts` |
| Location sharing while on the clock | shipped | Mounted on every page: while its owner has an open punch, fixes go to `/api/location` every 2 minutes, or sooner after moving 150 m (`fixDue`), with an "On the clock · sharing location" pill. RLS refuses a fix from anyone off the clock. In a browser, fixes come only while the CRM is on screen; inside the phone app they come from the background-location plugin with the phone locked, posted through the native HTTP stack (Android throttles WebView requests in the background). | `src/app/(app)/location-sharer.tsx`, `src/lib/time-clock/send-policy.ts`, `src/app/api/location/route.ts` |
| Automatic job arrivals | shipped | Each fix is checked against today's appointments for that person (job address, else the lead's), the production jobs assigned to them that are live today (In Progress, or Not Started with dates covering today — `jobIsLiveToday`), geocoded through the shared `address_geocode` cache (only what the Census geocoder actually answered is stored: a "not found" is rechecked after a day, an outage or 5-second timeout is never stored — `src/lib/geocode-cache.ts`), plus the office address. A job arrival carries `site_visits.job_id` (0185; until it runs, job arrivals just aren't logged). Inside the zone (default 150 m, plus up to 100 m of GPS accuracy) opens a site visit; leaving, clocking out or a break closes it. Visits are written by the server only. | `src/lib/time-clock/geo.ts`, `src/lib/data/time-clock.ts` |
| Team Map | shipped | Office/Admin: everyone on the clock on a Google map with a status each (at a job, in the office, driving, stopped away from a job, location off, on break), refreshed every minute from a thin route. Falls back to the list without a Maps key. | `src/app/(app)/team-map/`, `src/app/api/team-locations/route.ts`, `src/lib/time-clock/attendance.ts` |
| Timesheets | shipped | Office/Admin: a week (Mon–Sun, company zone) per person — hours per day, total, time on site (appointment and job visits), late arrivals, flags for overtime, missed clock-outs, no-shows, off-site clock-ins, clock-ins with no location, punches that skipped the location check, and office edits. Each punch shows where it was clocked in and out and the worker's reason for an off-site clock-in. Click a person to fix a punch (reason required); every fix is kept in the append-only `time_punch_changes` history. CSV export for payroll, with an off-site clock-ins column. | `src/app/(app)/timesheets/`, `src/lib/time-clock/punch-changes.ts` |
| Location check at clock-in | shipped | Every clock-in and clock-out through the app is stamped by the server with where it happened against the person's places today: at a place, how far from the nearest one, no location, or nothing on the map to check against (`clockStamp`). In "ask" mode a clock-in away from every place asks for a reason (quick picks, free text for "Something else") and then goes ahead; no location offers Try again or Clock in anyway. Nothing ever blocks a punch, and clock-outs and breaks are never questioned. The check has a 5-second budget so a slow geocoder can't hold up a punch. Only the service role writes the verdict: the 0185 trigger clears it on a worker's own insert and keeps it on a worker's update, so a punch made around the app shows as "Not checked". | `src/lib/time-clock/clock-in-check.ts`, `src/lib/time-clock/geo.ts`, `src/lib/actions/time-clock.ts`, `supabase/migrations/0185_clock_in_check.sql` |
| Settings › Time Clock & Tracking | shipped | Tracked roles, zone radius, office address, overtime line, late grace, auto clock-out limit, location-trail retention, and the location check at clock-in (off / record only / ask for a reason — default ask — and which roles, default Field and Production). Before 0185 runs the check block is disabled and the rest saves as before. | `src/app/(app)/settings/time-clock/`, `src/lib/time-clock/settings.ts` |
| Housekeeping cron | shipped | Hourly: a punch open past the auto clock-out limit is closed at the limit and flagged; location trails older than the retention are deleted. | `src/app/api/cron/time-clock/route.ts`, `.github/workflows/time-clock.yml` |
| Phone app (iOS/Android) | built; Android release build ready, not yet in the stores | A Capacitor shell (`mobile/`) that opens the live CRM (crm.aibuildpros.com) with the `@capacitor-community/background-geolocation` plugin, so sharing continues with the phone locked. Android shows an "On the clock" notification while sharing. The "Android App (test build)" GitHub Action builds an installable test APK; "Android App (Play release)" builds the signed bundle for Google Play from `main`, with a key made once by "Android upload key (create once)" (#086). Store releases need the Apple and Google developer accounts; the steps are in `docs/MOBILE_RELEASE.md` (no Mac needed — GitHub's Mac runners build iOS). | `mobile/`, `.github/workflows/android-app.yml`, `.github/workflows/android-release.yml`, `.github/workflows/android-upload-key.yml` |

Public `/privacy` and `/delete-account` pages, linked from the sign-in footer and the sidebar, back the store listing. | `src/components/web-only.tsx`, `src/app/privacy/`, `src/app/delete-account/`, `src/lib/app-store/` |
