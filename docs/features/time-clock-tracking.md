# Time Clock & Tracking

Clocking in and out, live location while on the clock, automatic arrivals at appointments, and the hours that go to payroll. Migration `0174_time_clock.sql`; decision #073.

| Feature | Status | Description | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time Clock | shipped | Everyone in a tracked role (Settings; default Sales, Field, Production, Office) clocks in, takes breaks and clocks out from their phone. First use shows a location notice that must be accepted. Shows hours today (a live stopwatch), where they are ("At Roof inspection · logged automatically") and today's timeline and schedule. A break ends the current punch, so breaks are unpaid without break arithmetic. | `src/app/(app)/time-clock/`, `src/lib/actions/time-clock.ts`, `src/lib/time-clock/hours.ts` |
| Location sharing while on the clock | shipped (browser) | Mounted on every page: while its owner has an open punch, the browser sends a fix every 2 minutes, or sooner after moving 150 m, to `/api/location`, and shows an "On the clock · sharing location" pill. RLS refuses a fix from anyone off the clock. A browser only reports while the CRM is on screen; the installable phone app (next phase) is what keeps it going in a pocket. | `src/app/(app)/location-sharer.tsx`, `src/app/api/location/route.ts` |
| Automatic job arrivals | shipped | Each fix is checked against today's appointments for that person (job address, else the lead's), geocoded through the shared `address_geocode` cache, plus the office address. Inside the zone (default 150 m, plus up to 100 m of GPS accuracy) opens a site visit; leaving, clocking out or a break closes it. Visits are written by the server only. | `src/lib/time-clock/geo.ts`, `src/lib/data/time-clock.ts` |
| Team Map | shipped | Office/Admin: everyone on the clock on a Google map with a status each (at a job, in the office, driving, stopped away from a job, location off, on break), refreshed every minute from a thin route. Falls back to the list without a Maps key. | `src/app/(app)/team-map/`, `src/app/api/team-locations/route.ts`, `src/lib/time-clock/attendance.ts` |
| Timesheets | shipped | Office/Admin: a week (Mon–Sun, company zone) per person — hours per day, total, time on site, late arrivals, flags for overtime, missed clock-outs, no-shows and office edits. Click a person to fix a punch (reason required); every fix is kept in the append-only `time_punch_changes` history. CSV export for payroll. | `src/app/(app)/timesheets/`, `src/lib/time-clock/punch-changes.ts` |
| Settings › Time Clock & Tracking | shipped | Tracked roles, zone radius, office address, overtime line, late grace, auto clock-out limit, location-trail retention. | `src/app/(app)/settings/time-clock/`, `src/lib/time-clock/settings.ts` |
| Housekeeping cron | shipped | Hourly: a punch open past the auto clock-out limit is closed at the limit and flagged; location trails older than the retention are deleted. | `src/app/api/cron/time-clock/route.ts`, `.github/workflows/time-clock.yml` |
| Background tracking (phone app) | planned | Wrap the CRM in an installable iOS/Android app with a background-location plugin so fixes keep coming with the screen locked. Needs Apple and Google developer accounts. | — |
| Week approval | planned | Lock an approved week before export. | — |
