| Marketing analytics | shipped | The marketing dashboard for a date window: five headline tiles with change vs. the previous period (leads created, appointments booked, signed contracts, cost per signed job, win rate as two rates in one tile: signed out of the period's leads and out of the leads that got an appointment set — the Dashboard's `win-rates.ts` helper, so the two pages agree — every tile opens what itemizes it), sources as a funnel (leads → appointments → estimates → signed → signed $, plus cost per sale and per lead) ranked by signed dollars with the sources that produced nothing folded under one line, a twelve-week strip of leads created and contracts signed, the sales team on the rep report's own definitions (appointments, show rate, estimates, signed, average job; a row expands to the rep's leads) with a signed contract counting for its Sales team seats (`sale-credit.ts`, DECISIONS #057), where the period's leads sit now by stage, and the latest signed contracts with days to close. **One revenue rule everywhere: a signed true contract** — change orders excluded, pipeline stage never counts; the leads at Won with no contract behind them are counted on the win-rate tile and open as a list.
