# Marketing & Analytics

Where leads come from and what they turned into, plus the Facebook Lead Ads and social-profile integrations that feed the pipeline.

| Feature | Status | Description | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing analytics | shipped | The marketing dashboard for a date window: five headline tiles with change vs. the previous period (leads created, appointments booked, signed contracts, cost per signed job, win rate — every tile opens what itemizes it), sources as a funnel (leads → appointments → estimates → signed → signed $, plus cost per sale and per lead) ranked by signed dollars with the sources that produced nothing folded under one line, a twelve-week strip of leads created and contracts signed, the sales team on the rep report's own definitions (appointments, show rate, estimates, signed, average job; a row expands to the rep's leads) with a signed contract counting for its Sales team seats (`sale-credit.ts`, DECISIONS #057), where the period's leads sit now by stage, and the latest signed contracts with days to close. **One revenue rule everywhere: a signed true contract** — change orders excluded, pipeline stage never counts; the leads at Won with no contract behind them are counted on the win-rate tile and open as a list. The tiles, sources, stages and latest contracts read the window's cohort (leads created in it); the team's appointments, estimates and contracts are dated in it. Every range is served by `marketing_analytics_rollup` (migration 0164) in one call — no lead rows ride to the browser, drill-downs fetch on expand — with a tested TypeScript mirror as the fallback until the migration runs. DECISIONS #056. | `src/app/(app)/marketing-analytics/`, `analytics-view.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/marketing-analytics.ts`, `src/lib/data/marketing-rollup.ts` (+ `.test.ts`), `src/components/charts/week-columns.tsx`, `src/lib/data/sale-credit.ts` (+ `.test.ts`), `supabase/migrations/0164_marketing_analytics_rollup.sql`, `0167_sale_credit_seats.sql` |
| Marketing spend by source | shipped | Settings › Lead sources › Spend by source: what each source cost in a calendar month, one amount per source per month (`marketing_spend`, migration 0165 — RLS: company members read, Office/Admin write). Marketing Analytics claims each month in proportion to the days the window covers (never a day that hasn't happened; All Time claims everything) and computes cost per lead over every lead the source produced and cost per sale over its signed contracts. Without spend a source falls back to `lead_cost` averaged over its priced leads — and when every priced lead carries the company default (0089 stamps $375 on every new lead), the page shows a dashed "default" chip instead of posing it as a measurement. | `src/app/(app)/settings/lead-sources/source-spend.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/marketing-spend.ts`, `src/lib/data/marketing-spend.ts` (+ `.test.ts`), `supabase/migrations/0165_marketing_spend.sql` |
| Bought-list sources | shipped | A per-source flag (`lead_sources.bought_list`, 0165) set on the same settings page. Marketing Analytics offers one chip, *Exclude bought lists*, that removes those sources' leads — and their appointments and documents — from every number at once, so a purchased cold list stops sitting in the same denominator as inbound channels. The chip appears only once a source is flagged. | `src/app/(app)/settings/lead-sources/source-spend.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/marketing-spend.ts`, `src/lib/data/marketing-rollup.ts` (`excludeSources`) |
| Rep performance report | shipped | Per-rep report over a date window: appointments held vs. logged-result rate, sellable estimates, revenue — a signed contract counts for the rep's Sales team seat on it, dollars split by share (`sale-credit.ts`, DECISIONS #057); an unsigned document follows whoever holds the lead (`effectiveEstimateRepId`). | `src/app/(app)/marketing-analytics/rep-report/` |
| Facebook Lead Ads integration | shipped | Per-company Meta Page connection (page id, access token, verify token, app secret) and a public leadgen webhook that creates leads from Facebook form submissions and fires the new-lead alert.
