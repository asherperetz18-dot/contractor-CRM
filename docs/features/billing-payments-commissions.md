# Billing, Payments & Commissions

Money in (customer payments, collections), money out (vendor bills), profit/loss, and the two separate commission schemes (dispatcher vs. sales rep).

| Feature | Status | Description | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bills to Pay | shipped | Cash-impact view of unpaid vendor bills — who's owed, what goes out this week, what's overdue against its planned date. RLS-gated to financial roles. | `src/app/(app)/bills/`, `src/lib/data/bills.ts`, `src/lib/actions/vendors.ts` |
| Payments | shipped | Signed-contract payment status across phases (paid/clearing/overdue/billed/unbilled), with a rollup collections summary. One search box narrows all three tables at once by contract #, customer, phase or amount (typed any way — "4500", "4,500", "$4,500.00"), plus status chips on the billed-progress list; stat cards stay company-wide. | `src/app/(app)/payments/`, `payments-view.tsx`, `payment-filters.ts` |
| Manual payment settle/remove | shipped | Payment history row tools for hand-recorded payments: "Mark cleared" turns a pending row (cheque taken, not banked) into the settled payment in place, and "Remove" (Office/Admin) deletes a mis-keyed or duplicate entry. Exists because the only way to settle a pending manual payment used to be recording it again — the same money then showed twice. Stripe rows get neither: they settle by webhook and are refunded in Stripe. | `src/app/(app)/payments/manual-payment-tools.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/manual-payments.ts`, `src/lib/data/manual-clear.ts` |
| Collect (receivables) | shipped | What's collectible right now vs. billable-but-not-yet-due, computed per estimate/lead from portal + manual payments. | `src/app/(app)/collect/`, `collect-view.tsx` |
| Profit & Loss | shipped | Per-job P&L: contract value, phases, payments, bills, bill payments and expenses rolled up; separately access-gated from general financial views (`canViewProfitLoss`). | `src/app/(app)/profit-loss/`, `src/lib/data/profit-loss.ts` |
| Dispatcher commissions | shipped | Percentage of gross sale paid to the dispatcher who brought the lead in. Office/Admin see everyone's; a dispatcher's own view is restricted to their own line, enforced in the server action rather than the page. | `src/app/(app)/commissions/`, `src/app/(app)/commissions/statement/`, `src/app/(app)/settings/dispatcher-commission/`, `src/lib/actions/dispatcher.ts` |
| Sales rep commissions | shipped | Rep's share of net profit (after lead cost and job spend), paid once a job is finished and settled. Deliberately kept on a separate page/scheme from dispatcher commissions — stacking them invited the two to be read as one number, and would have exposed the dispatcher scheme to reps. | `src/app/(app)/sales-commission/`, `rep-commission-table.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts` |
| Commission defaults | shipped | Company-wide starting commission/lead-cost/closer rates for new contracts; changing a default never retroactively changes an already-signed contract, since each contract stamps its own rate when its sales team is saved. | `src/app/(app)/settings/sales-commission/defaults-form.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/closer-defaults.ts` |
| Stripe portal payments | shipped | Each contractor connects their own Stripe account; keys are write-only in the UI (only last 4 chars ever round-trip to the browser) so no path exists to read out a live secret. | `src/app/(app)/settings/portal-payments/company-stripe.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/stripe-admin.ts`, `src/lib/crypto/secrets.ts` |
| Stripe diagnostics ("Stripe doctor") | shipped | Read-back of actual Stripe configuration plus a manual reconcile action for pending payments — exists because a payment that never posts looks identical from inside the app whether the customer abandoned checkout or the webhook simply never arrived (worse with ACH, which settles days later). | `stripe-doctor.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/stripe-admin.ts` |
| Stripe webhook handling | shipped | Verifies and applies Stripe events to payment records; shared logic between the platform-level and per-company webhook routes, since each contractor's own Stripe account signs with its own secret and the per-company route carries the company id in its URL so verification and row-scoping both key off it. | `src/app/api/stripe/webhook/`, `src/app/api/stripe/webhook/[companyId]/`, `src/lib/stripe/handle-webhook.ts` |
| Payment method resolution | shipped | Determines the actual payment method used on a Checkout session from the charge itself, not from the list of methods merely offered — `payment_method_types[0]` was previously misreported (e.g. a card payment logged as "klarna" purely due to list ordering). | `src/lib/stripe-method.ts` |
| Add-bill modal | shipped | Shared modal for adding a vendor bill against a job, used from both Bills and the crew project view. | `src/components/bills/add-bill-modal.tsx` |
