# Billing, Payments & Commissions

Money in (customer payments, collections), money out (vendor bills), profit/loss, and the two separate commission schemes (dispatcher vs. sales rep).

| Feature | Status | Description | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bills to Pay | shipped | Cash-impact view of unpaid vendor bills — who's owed, what goes out this week, what's overdue against its planned date. RLS-gated to financial roles. | `src/app/(app)/bills/`, `src/lib/data/bills.ts`, `src/lib/actions/vendors.ts` |
| Payments | shipped | Signed-contract payment status across phases (paid/clearing/partially paid/overdue/billed/unbilled). The Billed-Unpaid and Overdue cards sum each phase's unpaid remainder (`phaseOwedCents`) — the same per-phase arithmetic as Projects' "Owed to you" and Money to Collect — so a partly paid invoice shows "Partially paid" with what's still owed instead of vanishing into Paid, and the Payments and Projects headline figures always agree (DECISIONS #033). The same client and rep filters as Money to Collect (type-to-find client picker, rep dropdown, both URL-synced as `?client=&rep=`) narrow all three tables and the six stat cards at once (pick a client and every box is that client's money, same as Money to Collect) — this replaced the old free-text search box — plus status chips on the billed-progress list, which cut only that table, never the cards. | `src/app/(app)/payments/`, `payments-view.tsx`, `payment-filters.ts`, `src/components/ui/client-picker.tsx` |
| Manual payment settle/remove | shipped | Payment history row tools for hand-recorded payments: "Mark cleared" turns a pending row (cheque taken, not banked) into the settled payment in place, and "Remove" (Office/Admin) deletes a mis-keyed or duplicate entry. Exists because the only way to settle a pending manual payment used to be recording it again — the same money then showed twice. Stripe rows get neither: they settle by webhook and are refunded in Stripe. | `src/app/(app)/payments/manual-payment-tools.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/manual-payments.ts`, `src/lib/data/manual-clear.ts` |
| Manual payment edit + check # | shipped | The Payment history table shows a "Ck / ref #" column, and clicking a hand-recorded row opens an inline editor for amount, method, cheque/reference number, received date and note — before this the only fix for a typo was delete-and-record-again, which needs Office/Admin and loses `recorded_by`. The project report's "Payments received" statement prints the number next to the method ("check #1042") so it reconciles against the bank on paper. Stripe rows stay read-only: their record is what actually moved through Stripe. | `src/app/(app)/payments/edit-manual-payment.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/manual-payments.ts`, `src/lib/data/manual-edit.ts`, `src/app/(app)/projects/[id]/report/page.tsx` |

| Profit & Loss | shipped | Per-job P&L: contract value, phases, payments, bills, bill payments and expenses rolled up; separately access-gated from general financial views (`canViewProfitLoss`). | `src/app/(app)/profit-loss/`, `src/lib/data/profit-loss.ts` |
| Dispatcher commissions | shipped | Percentage of gross sale paid to the dispatcher who brought the lead in. Office/Admin see everyone's; a dispatcher's own view is restricted to their own line, enforced in the server action rather than the page. | `src/app/(app)/commissions/`, `src/app/(app)/commissions/statement/`, `src/app/(app)/settings/dispatcher-commission/`, `src/lib/actions/dispatcher.ts` |
| Sales rep commissions | shipped | Rep's share of net profit (after lead cost and job spend), paid once a job is finished and settled. Deliberately kept on a separate page/scheme from dispatcher commissions — stacking them invited the two to be read as one number, and would have exposed the dispatcher scheme to reps. | `src/app/(app)/sales-commission/`, `rep-commission-table.tsx`, `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts` |
| Commission links under Accounting | shipped | Both commission screens sit in the sidebar's Accounting group, after Payments and before Profit & Loss — commission is money the company owes out, so it lives with the rest of the money instead of as two loose links at the bottom of the menu. Page keys, routes, Role Visibility overrides and page-side access checks are all unchanged; someone who can see only a commission page sees the Accounting group holding just that link. | `src/lib/data/types.ts` (`PAGE_REGISTRY`), `src/lib/nav-groups.test.ts` |
| Closer commission seat | shipped | The closer holds their own seat on a signed contract (`estimates.closer_id` + `closer_pool_bp`, seeded at signature from the lead): their cut comes off the commission pool first and the two rep seats split what is left, so a job can pay two reps and a closer at once. The closer's line shows on `/sales-commission` under the same holds as a rep's. Contracts signed before migration 0153 keep their closer in rep seat two, untouched. | `supabase/migrations/0153_contract_closer_seat.sql`, `src/lib/data/types.ts` (`computeRepCommission`, `closerPoolShareBp`), `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts`, `src/app/(app)/estimates/[id]/sales-team.tsx` |
| Sales team change history | shipped | Append-only audit of a contract's commission seats: every Sales team save that changes anything (people, shares, rates) records who changed it, when, and what moved ("Salesperson: A → B"), shown to admins at the bottom of the panel. Exists because seats stay editable after signature and the statement computes live from whoever holds them now — a swap with no record restates pay history silently. | `supabase/migrations/0154_sales_team_changes.sql`, `src/lib/data/sales-team-changes.ts`, `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts` (`saveSalesTeam`, `getSalesTeamChanges`), `src/app/(app)/estimates/[id]/sales-team.tsx` |

See `docs/DECISIONS.md` #028. | `src/lib/data/sales-team-changes.ts` (`seatChangeError`, `seatHoldersChanged`), `src/lib/actions/rep-commission.ts` (`saveSalesTeam`), `src/app/(app)/estimates/[id]/sales-team.tsx` |
| Commission payouts & advances | shipped | The ledger of money actually handed to reps: Office/Admin record each payout — or an advance given before a job settled — with amount, date, optional job and note; every entry deducts from that rep's **balance due** (payable less paid, per salesperson, never netted across reps; paid past what qualified shows as "advanced ahead" and nets against their next settled job). `/sales-commission` gains Paid out / Balance due cards, a per-rep balance table and the ledger; the printable statement gains a Paid & advances section and closes with the balance, bank-statement style. Entries are remove-and-re-record (no edit); until migration 0158 is run the page renders exactly as before.
